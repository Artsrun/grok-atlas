## V3 rules

- No panels, chips, sliders, legends, tickers, badges, FIG numbers.
- One name on screen when a country or view is active.
- Hint (“tap a country”) dies after 4s.
- Tap `GROK.ATLAS` → instrument (`?v=2`).
- Crop corners only on v2.

## Not in this PR
